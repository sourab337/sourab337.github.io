About Me

Hello & jhu jhu!

I am Sourab, a Computer Science and Engineering student passionate about Natural Language Processing, Indigenous Language Technology, and Computational Linguistics. Currently serving as a Research Associate at the Institute of Policy Dynamics, I contribute to corpus collection and digitization efforts for underrepresented indigenous languages, with a primary focus on Chakma.

My current work centers on building computational resources for low-resource indigenous languages, including corpus development, language digitization, script recognition, and sentiment analysis, with the broader goal of extending these efforts to other indigenous languages of Bangladesh and beyond. I am also working on multicultural value alignment in Large Language Models (LLMs), exploring how AI systems can better reflect and respect diverse cultural and linguistic contexts.

I am also deeply interested in Social Computing and community-focused research, with a particular focus on the Chittagong Hill Tracts (CHT), spanning education, health, livelihood, policy, and history. Additional interests include Machine Learning, combining research and engineering to build practical, real-world solutions.

I aspire to build a career at the intersection of language technology, AI, and social research, advancing inclusive and community-driven approaches that support linguistically and socially underrepresented communities, particularly in the CHT region.

Open to research collaborations, academic partnerships, and opportunities in NLP, indigenous language technology, and CHT-focused research.
